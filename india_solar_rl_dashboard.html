Schulte et al. (2021) meta-analysis: Socio-demographic variables (age, gender, income) are NOT significantly correlated with adoption intention — but perceived benefits are. This means campaigns should focus on ROI messaging, not demographic targeting. Subjective norm (what neighbors do) has medium-large correlation, suggesting peer demonstration programs are effective.
